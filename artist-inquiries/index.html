Artist Inquiries

Get in touch.

If you're an artist or curator interested in collaborating with New Afro — exhibitions, pop-ups, residencies, or features in our journal — get in touch.

We accept rolling inquiries from artists, curators, and creative entrepreneurs whose practice resonates with our programme. If you would like to be considered for an upcoming exhibition, residency, pop-up, or feature in the journal, please send a short note about your work.

Email us at cheria@newafro.net or use the contact form. Please include:

  • A short paragraph about your practice
  • A link to your portfolio or website
  • The cities you are based in or travel to
  • What kind of collaboration you are looking for

We try to read everything that comes in, even when we cannot reply quickly.
