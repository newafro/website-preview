Privacy Policy

How New Afro handles personal data, cookies, and inquiries received through this site.

What we collect

New Afro respects your privacy. We collect only the information necessary to operate the site and respond to inquiries you send us — typically your name, email address, and the message you choose to share. We do not sell or trade personal data.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email is stored in our mailing provider's database and used solely to send you New Afro programming updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter we send.

Analytics & cookies

The site uses minimal cookies for essential functionality. We may use aggregate, anonymous analytics to understand which pages are read most; we do not track individual visitors across the web.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To make a request, write to cheria@newafro.net.
